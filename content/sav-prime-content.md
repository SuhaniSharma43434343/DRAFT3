## About SAV Prime General Trading LLC

SAV Prime General Trading LLC is a Dubai-based international trading and commodities company engaged in the sourcing, supply, and distribution of a diverse range of industrial raw materials, minerals, energy products, fertilizers, and polymers across global markets.

The company was originally established as Minara General Trading LLC in 2021/2022 and, after two years of operations and expansion, was rebranded as SAV Prime General Trading LLC. The transition reflected the company’s growing international presence, expanding product portfolio, and broader vision for global commodities trading.

What began with a strong focus on cement clinker and gypsum has evolved into a diversified commodities trading business with established networks across the Middle East, South Asia, and Africa.

Our core strength includes the sourcing and international movement of bulk commodities, particularly cement clinker and gypsum, through bulk carrier shipments. Depending on the commodity and destination, we also facilitate containerized and other forms of international transportation.

## Our Global Markets

Headquartered in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, SAV Prime has developed an international trading network connecting producers, suppliers, traders, importers, distributors, and end users across multiple regions.

Our key markets include:

- **Middle East:** UAE, Kuwait, Oman, Iraq, Bahrain, Saudi Arabia and Qatar
- **South Asia:** India, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh and Pakistan
- **Africa:** Guinea Conakry, Senegal, Ghana and Ivory Coast

In Africa, our activities include the sourcing and trading of metal scrap and raw cashew nuts, while our broader commodities network enables us to connect international sources of supply with established markets across Asia and the Middle East.
